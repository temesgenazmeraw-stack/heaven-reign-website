What we believe

Foundational beliefs

  1. The Bible is the inspired Word of God and our guide for life.
  2. There is one God, eternally Father, Son and Holy Spirit.
  3. Salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone.
  4. God still heals, delivers and restores today.
  5. Every believer is called to grow, to serve and to give.
  6. The Kingdom of Heaven transforms people and nations.

What happens at each gathering?

Wednesday Faith Clinic is Bible teaching with time for questions. Friday Healing Hours is worship and prayer for healing. Sunday Empowerment Hours is teaching and practical equipping for the week ahead.

What should I wear?

Whatever is comfortable. Come as you are.

Can someone pray with me?

Yes. The prayer team prays with anyone who asks, at every gathering. You can also send a request online.

Are children welcome?

Yes. Families are welcome at every gathering.

Blog

Words for the week

Faith Clinic

Faith that works on a Wednesday

Faith is not only for Sunday. It is how we answer the ordinary pressures of the middle of the week.

Read more

Hebrews 11:1 calls faith the substance of things hoped for. Substance is something you can stand on. This week, choose one promise of God and stand on it in one specific situation: a conversation, a bill, a decision. Bring your story to Faith Clinic.

Healing Hours

Bring the whole person

God cares for body, mind and spirit. Healing Hours is a place to bring all three.

Read more

In Mark 5, the woman who touched Jesus' garment received more than physical healing. He called her daughter and sent her away in peace. Come on Friday ready to receive prayer, and to pray for one another.

Empowerment Hours

Equipped to go

We gather on Sunday evening to be strengthened for the week ahead.

Read more

Ephesians 4:12 says leaders are given to equip the saints for the work of ministry. Empowerment Hours is about practical growth in prayer, in Scripture, and in serving the people God has placed around you.
